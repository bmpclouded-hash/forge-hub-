LIVE READOUT
RECENT EVENTS
LEARN MY HAND
Every hand is different. FORGE measures your open hand, fist and pinch, then sets the three sensitivity sliders for you. Takes about 30 seconds. Nothing is recorded: only finger-bend numbers are kept, and only until you press Apply.
GESTURES
MY GESTURES
Teach FORGE your own hand shapes (poses) and movements (motions). They only ever trigger safe actions you choose, and start out set to "Do nothing". Recordings stay on this device.
COMBOS
Chain your own gestures: do A, then B (then C…) within a few seconds to trigger one action. A wrong gesture in the middle cancels the combo. Combos are much harder to trigger by accident than single gestures.
